@conjureos/ui

ConjureOS UI

Every primitive the package ships, in all nine themes and both flavors. This page loads dist/ui.css, so what you see is what your app gets.

Theme

Buttons shipping

Every variant reads --cui-accent and --cui-on-accent. Six of the nine themes have a lead hue too light for a white label, so the label is a token rather than a literal.
